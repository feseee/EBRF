The purpose

Understanding over persuasion.

EBRF is a domain-independent reference architecture for disciplined reasoning. Its ChatGPT implementation is designed to evaluate an argument on the quality of its evidence and logic, consider credible alternatives, and make uncertainty visible.

It scales the depth of analysis to the question. A simple question can receive a direct answer; a complex decision calls for closer examination of assumptions, tradeoffs, and the consequences of being wrong.

What it helps you do

01 / EVIDENCE

Assess a claim

Examine source reliability, relevance, methodology, independence, and conflicting findings.

02 / ALTERNATIVES

Compare explanations

Consider plausible interpretations and distinguish facts from assumptions, inference, and speculation.

03 / CONFIDENCE

Make uncertainty useful

Match confidence to evidence and identify the information most likely to change a conclusion.

Start a conversation

Bring a claim, a choice, or an argument.

Once you have access to the plugin in ChatGPT, select EBRF Reasoning Assistant and try a prompt such as:

Evaluate this claim using EBRF. Separate established facts from assumptions and explain what evidence would change the conclusion.
Compare these options against my goals and constraints. Explain the evidence, tradeoffs, and uncertainty behind your recommendation.
Identify the weaknesses in my argument. Consider credible alternative explanations and suggest what additional evidence I need.

These are example prompts, not measured performance claims. The plugin is currently private; a public installation link will be added after publication.

The method

Nine stages.
One clear answer.

  1. Understand the problem
  2. Generate hypotheses
  3. Evaluate evidence
  4. Separate facts from reasoning
  5. Calibrate confidence
  6. Synthesize
  7. Form recommendations
  8. Verify
  9. Validate

The stages guide the analysis. Responses summarize the relevant evidence and reasoning without mechanically displaying every stage.

Foundation release

EBRF 1.0

The plugin includes the Charter, Manifesto, Runtime Instructions, Specification, Companion Guide, and release Read-Me. Version 1.0 is a frozen foundation release; changes to its principles, behavior, or architecture require a major framework version.

EBRF is a reasoning aid, not a guarantee of correctness. Check important claims and citations, and seek appropriate professional input for consequential decisions.
